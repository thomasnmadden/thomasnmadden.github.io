I am an undergraduate in mathematics at UC San Diego. My research interests are in sampling, high-dimensional probability, and optimization.

I am particularly interested in the interaction between probability, geometry, and optimization in the design and analysis of algorithms for sampling and large-scale computational problems.

Research

UC San Diego Mathematics Honors Program

2026–present

Acceleration for randomized Kaczmarz and randomized low-rank approximation. Advised by Robert J. Webber.

UCLA Computational and Applied Mathematics REU

Summer 2026

Studied convergence of Fisher-Rao and Wasserstein-Fisher-Rao gradient flows, initialization effects for sampling from mixture distributions, and conditions for convergence. Advised by Yifan Chen.

Scalable Optimization and Control Lab, UC San Diego

2025–present

Developed an acceleration method for the proximal bundle method in convex optimization using an iterate-acceptance procedure that permits large stepsizes. Advised by Yang Zheng.

Polymath Jr.

Summer 2025

Worked on random matrices and Λ-distributions, including an exact criterion for independence of classical random variables obtained from Λ-random variables. Advised by Sean Howe.
