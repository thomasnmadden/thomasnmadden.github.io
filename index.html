About

I am an undergraduate in mathematics at UC San Diego. I'm broadly interested in probability theory, optimization, and the mathematics of machine learning.

This past summer, I worked with Yifan Chen on establishing sharp convergence guarantees for Fisher-Rao and Wasserstein-Fisher-Rao gradient flows as a participant in the UCLA Computational and Applied Mathematics (CAM) REU. Previously, I worked on acceleration for the proximal bundle method in the SOC Lab with Yang Zheng and on eigenvalue statistics for various random matrix groups with Sean Howe. I'm also doing an honors thesis in mathematics with Rob Webber on randomized numerical linear algebra.

Outside of math, I like watching movies, drinking coffee, fashion, and traveling.
